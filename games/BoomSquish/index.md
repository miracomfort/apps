# Game BoomSquish

BoomSquish is a playful 2D physics platformer where every explosion sends your soft, squishy hero flying through dangerous levels.

## Key Features

- Bomb-powered movement
- Fun soft-body physics
- Challenging obstacles and lava hazards
- Limited bombs and strategic cooldowns
- Checkpoints and locally saved progress
- Simple mobile controls for iPhone and iPad
- Offline play with no accounts required
- No ads, in-app purchases, or tracking
