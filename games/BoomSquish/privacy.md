# Privacy Policy for BoomSquish

## 🇬🇧 English

**Effective date: 22 August 2026**

BoomSquish respects your privacy. The game is designed to work without collecting, transmitting, or sharing your personal data.

## 2. Information We Collect

BoomSquish does not collect personal information or transmit personal information to the developer or to third-party services.

In particular, the game does not:

- require an account or sign-in;
- collect names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, IP addresses, contacts, photos, audio, or other personal information;
- use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or profiling;
- use the advertising identifier or track you across apps or websites;
- contain in-app purchases or subscriptions; or
- request access to device permissions such as location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, Bluetooth, motion data, or notifications.

## 3. Game Progress Stored on Your Device

BoomSquish stores limited game-progress information locally on your device, such as completed levels or checkpoints. This information is used only to let you continue your game.

The progress information:

- remains on your device;
- is not sent to the developer or any third party;
- is not associated with an account or identity; and
- can be reset using the reset or new-game function in the game.

Deleting BoomSquish from your device will normally delete its locally stored game data. Your operating system or device backup settings may independently retain a backup of app data. BoomSquish does not itself upload or synchronize game progress through iCloud or any other cloud service.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Apple Platform Data

BoomSquish does not include third-party advertising, analytics, cloud-save, social-networking, or crash-reporting services.

Apple may independently process information connected with downloading and using apps, including App Store activity and, depending on your device and privacy settings, diagnostic information. This processing is controlled by Apple and is subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 5. Children’s Privacy

BoomSquish is a general-audience game suitable for players of all ages. Because the game does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Contacting the Developer

If you contact the developer by email, the information contained in your message will be used only to answer your request and handle any related support or privacy matter. The legal basis for this limited processing, where the GDPR applies, is the developer's legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Email correspondence will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request and meet any applicable legal obligations. It will not be sold or used for advertising.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Where applicable law, including the GDPR, applies to information you provide when contacting the developer, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or data portability, and to object to processing. You may exercise these rights by contacting us.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data-protection supervisory authority responsible for your place of residence or with the competent German supervisory authority.

Because BoomSquish itself does not collect personal data, there is normally no in-game personal data for the developer to access, provide, correct, or delete.

## 8. Security and International Transfers

Because BoomSquish does not transmit personal information to the developer, the developer does not maintain a server-side database of player information and does not transfer player information internationally.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game’s features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
